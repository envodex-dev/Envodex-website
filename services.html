Capabilities

Comprehensive Facade Engineering Services

We deliver end-to-end technical engineering packages tailored to the unique architectural, structural, and environmental challenges of every building envelope.

Whether designing ultra-high performance unitized curtain walls, complex geometric steel support structures, or thermally optimized cladding assemblies, our calculations provide rigorous verification and peace of mind.

Specializations

Our Technical Services Scope

1. Facade Structural Engineering & FEA

Complete structural calculations for unitized curtain walls, stick systems, window walls, stone rainscreens, and composite metal panels. Non-linear plate bending and finite element verification using Strand7.

2. Structural Glass Engineering

Deflection and stress analysis for monolithic, laminated, and insulated glass units using SJ Mepla. Point-fixed spider fittings, glass fins, balustrades, and walkable glass floors per ASTM E1300 and prEN 19100.

3. Connection Design & Anchor Channel Verification

Detailed verification of top wind brackets, bottom dead-load brackets, hook systems, splice joints, and cast-in anchor channels (Hilti / Halfen) under combined 3D tension, shear, and moment actions.

4. Louver & Shading System Analysis

Thickness optimization, wind load resistance, vortex shedding, and natural frequency verification to prevent acoustic whistle and fatigue failure under extreme wind gusts.

5. Envelope Thermal & Condensation Modeling

Two-dimensional thermal bridging analysis via LBNL THERM to quantify U-values, condensation temperature index (fRsi), and compliance with building energy codes.

6. Peer Review & Value Engineering

Independent structural third-party auditing, extrusion section weight reduction, glass thickness rationalization, and value engineering without compromising architectural aesthetics or safety.
