About Envodex

Independent Facade Structural Consultancy

Envodex Consulting Engineers is a specialized structural engineering consultancy focused exclusively on the building envelope and facade sector. Led by experienced engineers with years of international project deliveries across the UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Europe, we assist architectural practices, Tier-1 facade contractors, and structural consultants in engineering safe, durable, and energy-efficient building enclosures.

Our consulting practice operates across all development phases: conceptual system feasibility, tender stage optimization, detailed finite element calculations, connection bracket verification, third-party peer review, and on-site engineering quality assurance.

Why Choose Envodex

Core Engineering Advantages

Specialist Analytical Expertise

Mastery of non-linear structural modeling, plate stress analysis, glass post-breakage behavior, and 3D space frames using Strand7, STAAD.Pro, and SJ Mepla.

International Code Compliance

Direct access to seasoned facade structural engineers ensuring swift turnaround times, responsive calculation iterations, and dedicated client service.
