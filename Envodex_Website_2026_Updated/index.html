Precision Building Envelope & Facade Consultancy

We combine structural rigor, constructability, and energy-efficiency to deliver envelope solutions that last. From early design-stage consultancy and section optimization to detailed finite element analysis (FEA), shop drawings, and site-level quality verification, Envodex works seamlessly alongside architects, tier-1 facade contractors, and building owners to achieve high-performance building envelopes.

Our team specializes in unitized curtain walls (UCW), stick framing systems, complex glass balustrades, walkable skylights, shading louvers, and bespoke bracketry connection checks.

Code Compliance

International Design Standards

Envodex designs, calculates, and verifies facade components in strict compliance with the world's most rigorous engineering codes and specifications.

United Kingdom & Middle East

CWCT Standards

Center for Window and Cladding Technology standard specification for building envelopes, rainscreens, and curtain walls.

  • Standard for Systemised Building Envelopes
  • Dynamic weathertightness, water & air permeability
  • Hard/soft body impact resistance & safety retention
  • Thermal bridging & moisture condensation mitigation
European Structural Codes

Eurocodes (EN)

Harmonized technical rules for structural design of buildings and civil engineering works across Europe and global projects.

  • EN 1991-1-4: Wind actions & local pressure coefficients
  • EN 1993: Design of structural steel framing & brackets
  • EN 1999 (Eurocode 9): Aluminum structural design
  • prEN 19100 / EN 16612: Structural design of glass
  • EN 1992-4: Fastener anchoring in concrete
American Standards

ASTM International

American Society for Testing and Materials standards for materials, glazing resistance, and facade testing protocols.

  • ASTM E1300: Load resistance of glass in buildings
  • ASTM E2751: Design and performance of walkable glass
  • ASTM E330: Structural performance by uniform static air pressure
  • AAMA 501: Exterior facade dynamic water penetration methods
